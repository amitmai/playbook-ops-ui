# Fonts

Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits
redistribution with the software. They are self-hosted rather than loaded from a
third party: this tool is used with a token that can read a private repository,
and a font request would tell that third party the origin and time of every use.

- **Courier Prime** — Alan Dague-Greene for Quote-Unquote Apps. Designed as a
  better Courier specifically for screenplays, which is exactly why it is here:
  a promptbook is typed in Courier, so this is the material rather than a
  costume. https://quoteunquoteapps.com/courierprime/
- **Archivo** — Omnibus-Type. A grotesque drawn for high performance in
  headlines and small print, which is what a call sheet is.
  https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo

OFL 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
