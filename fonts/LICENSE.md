# Fonts

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which
permits redistribution with the software. They are self-hosted rather than
loaded from a third party: this tool is used with a token that can read a
private repository, and a font request would tell that third party the origin
and time of every use.

- **Frank Ruhl Libre** — Yanek Iontef, based on Frank-Rühl (Rafael Frank, 1908),
  the first Hebrew serif of the modern era, redrawn for Haaretz. Latin and
  Hebrew subsets are both here, so a Hebrew client name sets in the same face
  rather than falling back. It carries the voice: headings, client names, the
  one thing to do, and the playbook's advice.
  https://github.com/impallari/Frank-Ruhl-Libre
- **Archivo** — Omnibus-Type. A grotesque drawn for high performance in
  headlines and small print, which is what the chrome of this tool is.
  https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo
- **Courier Prime** — Alan Dague-Greene for Quote-Unquote Apps. Designed as a
  better Courier for screenplays. Here it carries the record: dates, counts,
  issue numbers and attempt counts, because a record of what happened is typed.
  https://quoteunquoteapps.com/courierprime/
